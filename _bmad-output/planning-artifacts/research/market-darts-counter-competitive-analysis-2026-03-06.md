## Differentiated Features (Your Unique Selling Points)

1. **No Account Required** - DartCounter requires login; you win on frictionless entry
2. **ElevenLabs Premium Voice** - None of the competitors have this
3. **Pro Player AI Variants** - Simulate specific pro players (Phil Taylor, MVG, etc.)
4. **Session-only Statistics** - Others push cloud accounts; you keep it simple and private
5. **Trust-based Editing** - Unique "gentleman's sport" philosophy - collaborative editing without permissions
6. **Tournament CSV Export/Import** - Rare feature in this space
7. **PartyKit Real-time Sync** - Modern, fast infrastructure

## Market Validation

### Positive Signals

- Space is not oversaturated
- No app perfectly occupies "premium but simple" niche
- Web-based approach is right (mobile = app store friction)
- Announcer + AI combo is differentiated
- Growing interest in casual competitive games

### Risks to Consider

- DartCounter has massive market presence (1M+ downloads)
- Many free options already exist
- Differentiation is subtle, not radical
- Need strong word-of-mouth for growth (if that matters)

## Conclusion

**Build with confidence.** The darts counter app space has room for a well-executed, frictionless web app with premium touches (ElevenLabs voice, pro AI variants). Your "goldilocks" position (better than barebone, simpler than social platforms) is viable.

The key success factors:
1. Execute well on core UX (smooth scoring)
2. Nail the announcer experience
3. Make multiplayer frictionless (room codes)
4. Keep it simple - no account, instant play
